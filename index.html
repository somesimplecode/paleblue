Meet Dot. The pill that lets your care team know you're okay.

Dot is a tiny sensor inside a standard capsule. When it reaches your stomach, it sends a signal to a soft patch on your skin, letting your care team know your medication was taken. No logs. No guesswork. Just peace of mind after transplant.

Important safety information

Dot is contraindicated in patients with known or suspected gastrointestinal strictures, fistulas, obstruction, or inflammatory bowel disease, including Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. Capsule retention may occur and may require endoscopic or surgical removal.

Do not undergo MRI until passage of the capsule has been confirmed. Seek medical attention for abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, or difficulty swallowing. Adherence data supports, and does not replace, clinical judgment.
